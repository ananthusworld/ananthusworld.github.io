About

SRE Academy is an educational platform focused on practical reliability skills. Our goal is to help students, developers, and platform teams apply SRE principles with confidence in production environments.

Reliability Philosophy

Reliability is not a tool purchase. It is a team habit built through clear service-level objectives, operational discipline, and continuous learning.

  • Measure health from user experience, not only infrastructure signals.
  • Use playbooks and progressive delivery to reduce incident blast radius.
  • Close every incident with blameless postmortems and systemic fixes.
